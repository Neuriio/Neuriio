# Feed and Points

## Feed

The **Feed** is where creators and holders talk about the tokens they back.

* **Send $** on any post sends the poster any token you hold on Elysium, straight to their Neuriio wallet.
* Live trades for each token show on its page in **Explore**.

{% hint style="info" %}
Posting to the feed is coming soon. Until then, the feed shows sample posts.
{% endhint %}

## Points

Points reward activity on Neuriio. You earn them by:

* **Trading** launched tokens, on the curve or in their pools.
* **Launching** your own token.
* **Staking** X tokens and Neuriio.

Points are already being counted for every trade and launch. The **Points** panel and a public leaderboard are coming soon. Point rates can change; changes apply going forward only and never remove points you've already earned.

Points are a measure of activity on Neuriio. They are not a token and have no promised value.
