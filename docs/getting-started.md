# Getting Started

## 1. Log In With X

Open [app.neuriio.xyz](https://app.neuriio.xyz) and choose **Account → Login**. X is the only way to log in: your X account is your identity on Neuriio.

## 2. Neuriio Wallet

When you first log in, Neuriio creates a wallet for you. You don't need to install anything or manage a seed phrase.

* **Your address** is at the top of the Account panel. Click it to copy.
* **Your balances** on Elysium are listed below it.
* **Export private key** at the bottom of the panel opens a secure window where you can take your key to any other wallet. Never share it.

## 3. Deposit USDC and HYPE

Every trade on Neuriio is priced in **USDC** and you need **HYPE** on Elysium for gas. Send them on **Elysium** to your Neuriio address.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Send on Elysium testnet only. Funds sent on another network to this address won't show up in Neuriio.
{% endhint %}

## 4. Launch, Trade, Send

* [Launch your token](launching/launch-your-token.md) from the Launch page.
* Buy and sell any token from Explore. See [Trading and fees](trading/trading-and-fees.md).
* Stake any token launched through Neuriio for a share of its trading fees, and earn Neuriio. See [Staking](trading/staking.md).
* **Send** any token you hold to any address on Elysium from the Account panel (**Send $**). Double-check the address: sends can't be undone.
