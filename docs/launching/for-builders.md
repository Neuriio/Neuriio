# For builders

Teams building real products face a hard choice when they launch a token: raise from insiders and hand them cheap
supply, or launch in public and risk snipers and liquidity that can be pulled. Neuriio gives builders a third option: a
**fair launch with liquidity locked forever**.

## What a builder gets

* **A fair start.** No presale, no team or insider allocation, no whitelist. Your community buys on the same curve, at
  the same price, as everyone else.
* **No sniping the pool.** Only Neuriio's launchpad can open your token's pool, and it does so in the same transaction
  that sells the last curve token.
* **Liquidity nobody can drain.** At graduation, all the USDC your curve raised goes into your token's pool, and the LP
  tokens are burned. The liquidity is locked forever.
* **Ongoing creator fees.** Once your token graduates, your account earns 10% of its pool fees, in USDC. See
  [Creator fees](../trading/creator-fees.md).
* **Staking for your community.** Holders can stake your token to share 40% of its pool fees. See
  [Staking](../trading/staking.md).

## How to launch as a company

Launch through your **company's X account**, the same way an individual launches. The token is named after that
account (for example `@yourcompany`, ticker `yourcompany`), and each X account can launch once. Use your profile to
say what you're building and link to your product.

## What the token is, and isn't

{% hint style="danger" %}
A Neuriio token is **not** equity, shares, debt or a claim on your company, its revenue or its assets. Don't describe it
that way, and don't promise holders returns, buybacks or price targets.
{% endhint %}

* The USDC your curve raises goes **into your token's locked pool**, not to your company. Neuriio is not a way to raise
  money.
* What your company earns is its creator share of trading fees.
* Follow the [creator rules](../trust/creator-rules.md), and check your local rules before launching.
