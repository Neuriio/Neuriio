# For builders

Teams building real products face a hard choice when they launch a token: raise from insiders and hand them cheap
supply, or launch in public and risk snipers. Neuriio gives builders a fair launch tied to their real identity.

## What a builder gets

* **A fair start.** No presale, no team or insider allocation, no whitelist. Your community buys in the same pool, on
  the same terms, as everyone else.
* **One token, tied to your X account.** Each X account can launch once, so your token is the only one launched
  through Neuriio under your name.
* **Staking for your community.** Holders can stake your token from launch to share 40% of Neuriio's fee on its trades,
  in USDC, and earn Neuriio. See [Staking](../trading/staking.md).

## How to launch as a company

Launch through your **company's X account** and pick the **Founders** category. The token is named after that account
(for example `@yourcompany`, ticker `yourcompany`), and each X account can launch once. Use your profile to say what
you're building and link to your product.

## What the token is, and isn't

{% hint style="danger" %}
A Neuriio token is **not** equity, shares, debt or a claim on your company, its revenue or its assets. Don't describe it
that way, and don't promise holders returns, buybacks or price targets.
{% endhint %}

* Buying your token doesn't send money to your company. Neuriio is not a way to raise money.
* Follow the [creator rules](../trust/creator-rules.md), and check your local rules before launching.
