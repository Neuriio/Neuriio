# Launch an FTF

An **FTF** ("Friends Turned Fam") is one token launched by **exactly four people** together. It launches and trades on
Ascend the same way as a personal token.

## How it works

1. **The founder creates the FTF** on the Launch page: a name and a ticker (2–10 letters or numbers, unique among
   FTFs).
2. **The founder invites three members.** Each member accepts the invite from their notifications.
3. **The founder sets the details**: a category, a profile and an optional picture.
4. **Launch.** All four members are fixed on-chain at launch and can't be changed later.

## Rules

* Exactly four members, founder included, each with their own X account.
* Being in an FTF doesn't use up anyone's personal launch, and one person can be in more than one FTF.
* The FTF's creator fees are split equally: **25% to each member**. See
  [Creator fees](../trading/trading-and-fees.md#creator-fees).
* FTFs follow the same [creator rules](../trust/creator-rules.md) as personal tokens.
