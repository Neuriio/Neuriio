# Trading and Fees

Tokens launched through Neuriio live on **Ascend**, the launchpad on Elysium. Each token trades in its Ascend pool, in
**USDC**, from the moment it launches. You can trade it from its page in Explore, on Ascend, or through any app or
aggregator that routes to Ascend's pools.

## The fee

Every trade of a token launched through Neuriio pays **1.5%**:

| Part | Fee | Goes to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ascend | 1% | Ascend, under its own rules |
| Neuriio | 0.5% | Shared out as below |
| **Total** | **1.5%** | |

The fee is charged by the pool, so it applies to every trade on any site, including bots and aggregators. Trading
through Neuriio costs nothing extra.

## Where Neuriio's 0.5% goes

| Share | Of the 0.5% | Of each trade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People staking that token | 40% | 0.2% |
| KNTQ buyback | 40% | 0.2% |
| Treasury | 20% | 0.1% |

* **Token stakers** are people who stake the token that was traded, shared by how much each has staked. See
  [Staking](staking.md).
* **KNTQ buyback:** USDC used to buy back KNTQ, the Kinetiq token.
* **Treasury:** Neuriio's own funds.

If nobody is staking a token, its stakers' share goes to the treasury.

Neuriio's share is collected in USDC and paid out to each token's stakers regularly.

## Protection on every trade

* **Slippage limit.** You set the minimum you'll accept. If the price moves past it before your trade lands, the trade
  cancels.
* **Deadline.** A trade that isn't included in time cancels instead of filling later at a different price.

## Costs you pay

* The 1.5% trading fee.
* A small amount of HYPE for gas on Elysium.

The first time you buy, and the first time you sell a token, your wallet also approves that token for trading. It
happens automatically and costs gas once.

There's no fee to launch, stake, send tokens or claim, beyond gas.
