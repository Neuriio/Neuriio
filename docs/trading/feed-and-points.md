# Home and Neuriio

## Home

**Home** is where creators, holders and the community talk about the tokens they back. It's also the place for
questions about Neuriio: post them there.

* **Post** once you're logged in with X.
* **Send $** on any post sends the poster any token you hold on Elysium, straight to their Neuriio wallet.
* **Portfolio** shows your balance across your Neuriio wallet and any wallets you add, and your tokens on Elysium.
* Live trades for each token show on its page in **Explore**.

## Neuriio

Neuriio is earned only by staking: **100 Neuriio for every $1** you earn from staking, up to 1,000,000,000 in total.
See [Staking](staking.md).

Your Neuriio and your badges show in the **Neuriio** panel at the top right.

Neuriio has no promised value.
