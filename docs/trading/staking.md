# Staking

Staking lets you earn trading fees of a token you hold, in USDC, and mine Neuriio.

| Stake                              | You earn                 |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Any token launched through Neuriio | 40% of that token's fees |

Staking is a choice. There's no lockup: stake or unstake any amount at any time.

## Staking a Token

Any token launched through Neuriio can be staked from the moment it launches, from the **stake card** on its Explore page.

* Every trade of that token, **40% of it goes to the people staking it**, shared by how much each has staked. See [Trading and fees](trading-and-fees.md).
* You can stake from your Neuriio wallet or from your own wallet.
* If nobody is staking a token, its stakers' share goes to the holders.

## Neuriio

Staking is the only way to earn Neuriio. For every **$1** you earn from staking, you earn **100 Neuriio**.

* Neuriio is capped at **1,000,000,000** in total. Once the cap is reached, staking keeps paying USDC but earns no more Neuriio.
* There's nothing to claim: your Neuriio is counted automatically and shows in the **Neuriio** panel at the top right.
* Your Neuriio unlocks **badges**, from the first at 1,000 Neuriio up to the highest at 2,500,000.

## Rewards Drip In

Fees don't land all at once. Each payment to a token's stakers drips out over **one hour** to whoever is staked during that hour. Jumping in for a moment before a big trade and leaving right after earns almost nothing; staying staked earns your full share. If everyone leaves partway through, the rest waits for the next stakers.

## Claiming

The **Claim** tab on a token's stake card shows the USDC you've earned on that token. Claim any time; you only pay gas. Unclaimed rewards never expire.

## Good To Know

* The staking contract has **no owner**: nobody, including Neuriio, can move staked tokens or rewards. Only you can unstake yours.
* Fees come from real trading activity. They go up and down with volume and can be zero.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Staking shares trading fees; it is not a promise of value or of any amount. Neuriio has no promised value. Tokens are memes and can go to zero. Read the [Disclaimer](../trust/disclaimer.md).
{% endhint %}
