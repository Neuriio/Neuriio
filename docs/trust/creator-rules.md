# Creator rules

Your token is a way for people to back you and what you do. These rules keep it that way. Profiles that break them can
be hidden from Neuriio.

## Do

* Be who you say you are. Your token is tied to your X account.
* Use your profile to say what you do and link to your real work.
* Talk about your token as a meme your community can back and trade.

## Don't

* **Promise returns.** No "this will 10x", price targets or "guaranteed" anything.
* **Promise pumps or buybacks.** Don't say you or anyone will buy to push the price up.
* **Present the token as an investment**, equity, shares, revenue share or a claim on you or your company.
* **Impersonate** a person, project or company.
* **Coordinate manipulation**, such as wash trading or organised pump-and-dumps.
* **Post illegal or harmful content** in your profile or on the feed.

## What happens if you break them

Neuriio can hide your profile and token from the app. Because tokens live on-chain, holders can still sell, and your
pool's liquidity stays locked.
